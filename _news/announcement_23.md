New paper in *Progress in Aerospace Sciences*: ["Liquid-propellant rocket engine health monitoring technologies: A survey, insights, and prospects"](https://authors.elsevier.com/a/1nx201LW8XLqA3). Authors: Jianjun Wu, Yuwei Liu, Yuqiang Cheng, Shuming Yang, and **Yang Li**. From a Physical AI perspective, this survey reframes liquid-propellant rocket-engine health monitoring as a physics-grounded, closed-loop intelligence problem—linking multimodal sensing, fault diagnosis, prognostics, and autonomous decision-making to make safety-critical propulsion systems more self-aware and resilient. **Progress in Aerospace Sciences** is Elsevier’s invitation-only journal devoted exclusively to critical reviews across aeronautics and astronautics—including AI, machine learning, and onboard autonomy—and currently reports a CiteScore of 34.8 and an Impact Factor of 16.2.
